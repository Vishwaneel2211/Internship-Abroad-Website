Internships in Germany

Germany is a structured, engineering-led economy. Students look here for technical depth, clear workplace processes, and experience inside European teams.

Apply for Germany

What students should know

Internships in Germany often sit in engineering, information technology, finance, and business operations. Cities students commonly ask about include Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt, and Hamburg.

Work culture tends to be planned and precise. That can suit students who want to learn how larger European organisations document work, review quality, and collaborate across teams.

Eligibility, language needs, and whether a role is paid depend on the employer and the specific opening. An internship does not guarantee a job or a visa.

Industry depth

Engineering, automotive, research, and technology employers are a frequent focus.

Workplace German

Some roles work in English. Others expect German. We confirm this per opening.

Documents vary

A resume and academic records are typical. Exact papers depend on the employer.
