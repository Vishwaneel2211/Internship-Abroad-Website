Inside this program

Engineering internships are for students who want site, lab, or project-office exposure in a technical discipline.

Work might include drawings support, measurements, documentation, or assisting a project engineer. The discipline (civil, mechanical, electrical, and others) depends on the opening.

Safety rules of the workplace apply in full. An internship is supervised experience, not an unsupervised professional licence.

Seeing how a technical task is specified, checked, and recorded.

Site awareness

Documentation

Reports and mark-ups that another engineer can review.
