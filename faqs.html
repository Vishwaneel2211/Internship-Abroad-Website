Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply for international internships?

Recent graduates, final-year students and young professionals can enquire. Eligibility depends on the specific opportunity and destination.

Which countries do you offer internship opportunities in?

We focus on Germany, the UAE, Singapore and GCC countries.

What internship domains are available?

Domains include IT, software, business, digital marketing, finance, HR, engineering, and hospitality and tourism. Availability varies by opportunity.

Do I need previous work experience?

Not always. Requirements differ by role and employer, and our team can tell you what applies to your profile.

What documents are required?

Commonly a resume, academic records and ID. Exact requirements depend on the opportunity and destination, and we will confirm them with you.

Does your company assist with the application process?

Yes, we offer guidance and application assistance. Final decisions rest with employers and the relevant authorities.

Are international internships paid or unpaid?

It varies by employer, country and role. Compensation is confirmed per opportunity and is not guaranteed.

Does an internship guarantee a job or work visa?

No. An internship builds experience but does not guarantee employment or a visa. Visa rules depend on the destination country and your circumstances.
