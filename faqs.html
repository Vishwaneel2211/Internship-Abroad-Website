Frequently asked

About our internships

Who can apply for an international internship?

Final-year students, recent graduates and young professionals are welcome to apply. Eligibility may vary based on the destination and role.

What countries can I choose from?

Our opportunities currently include Germany, the UAE, Singapore and selected GCC countries. Availability varies by career field and internship date.

What internship fields are available?

Options commonly include IT, software development, business, marketing, finance, human resources, engineering and hospitality. Available placements depend on the program.

Do I need previous work experience?

Not always. Requirements differ by role and host organization. We assess your profile and will explain the requirements for suitable opportunities.

Frequently asked

Internship application process

How do I start the application process?

Choose your preferred destination and career field, then submit the application form. Our team will review your details and contact you about suitable next steps.

What happens after I submit my application?

We review your profile for suitability and availability. If appropriate, we discuss the opportunity and begin placement planning if you decide to proceed.

Can I change my application details later?

Yes. Before confirmation, you can update your destination, program, preferred dates or career field by contacting our team.

What documents will I need?

Requirements vary by opportunity, but you may need a résumé, academic records, identification and a short profile about your goals and experience.

Frequently asked

Program fees and payments

Are internships paid or unpaid?

Payment terms vary by employer, destination and role. Compensation is confirmed for each opportunity and should not be assumed unless stated in writing.

What costs should I plan for?

Costs can include program fees, travel, insurance, visas and personal expenses. The team provides a clear breakdown for the specific internship you select.

Are installment payments available?

Payment plans may be available depending on the program and timing. Ask our team for the current options before confirming your placement.

Frequently asked

Preparing for your internship

Do you help with travel and accommodation?

We provide guidance on practical arrangements and explain what is included in your selected program. Support is tailored to the destination and opportunity.

Do I need to speak the local language?

English is commonly used for many opportunities. Some roles may require local-language skills, and these requirements will be clearly stated.

Can I intern with a friend or university group?

In some destinations and programs, group or paired placements may be possible. Contact us early so we can discuss availability and matching options.

Frequently asked

Your internship experience

Does an internship guarantee employment?

No. Internships provide professional experience and exposure, but employment is subject to future vacancies and employer decisions.

Will I receive a certificate or reference?

Where available, we support completion documentation and references. The exact documents provided depend on the host organization and program.

Does an internship guarantee a work visa?

No. Visa eligibility and approval are determined by the destination’s authorities. We provide guidance, but final decisions belong to the relevant government.
