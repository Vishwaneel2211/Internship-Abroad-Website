Inside this program

Business internships sit close to how an office actually runs: coordination, simple analysis, and follow-up on ongoing work.

Students may prepare meeting notes, track tasks, compare options for a small decision, or support a manager’s weekly reporting.

The role is observational and supportive. You will not be the decision-maker, and the experience does not promise a management job afterwards.

Operations

How work moves from request to completion inside a team.

Light analysis

Summarising numbers or options so someone else can decide.

Professional habits

Deadlines, email tone, and working with people outside your course.
