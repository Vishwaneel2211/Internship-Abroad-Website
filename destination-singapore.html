Internships in Singapore

Singapore is a compact Asia-Pacific hub for finance, technology, trade, and professional services.

Apply for Singapore

What students should know

Internship interest here often covers finance, software, business operations, and logistics. English is the main working language in most professional offices.

The city-state is highly regulated and internationally connected. Students see how regional headquarters coordinate work across Southeast Asia.

Openings are selective. Previous coursework or a relevant project can matter as much as formal work experience, and outcomes are never guaranteed.

Finance and trade

Banks, professional services, and trading firms are common areas of interest.

Technology teams

Software and IT roles are frequently requested by students.

No visa promise

Permission to intern depends on the employer and Singapore’s rules at the time.
